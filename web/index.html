About me

The person behind the work.

I'm Samuel, a computer engineer, web developer and graphic designer from Nigeria. I started with flyers and fell in love with the web. Every site I make is designed and coded by me, from the first sketch to launch day.

I've built for restaurants, churches, schools, a sneaker brand and a cleaning company in the UK. Whatever you do, I'll make it look premium.

  • Web Developer & Programmer

    JavaScript, React and Next.js; sites, portals and web apps coded by hand.

  • Computer Engineer

    Trained in how hardware and software work together, from circuits to code.

  • Graphic Designer

    Founder of Yeni Graphics: flyers, branding and visual identity.

  • 3D & Motion Designer

    Three.js and Framer Motion for scenes and scroll effects that feel premium.
